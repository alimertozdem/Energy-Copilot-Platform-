"Gap to median" is that building's intensity above the median for its own use type (or the estate median where a type has fewer than three buildings), multiplied by its own floor area and your tariff. Like is compared with like: a laboratory is not wasteful for using more than an office. For buildings at or below the median it is zero — this report never counts a saving twice, and never claims a building could go below what your own estate has shown is achievable.

What this report does not tell you

Worth saying before anyone acts on the ranking.

A high intensity is not automatically waste. A building full of servers, kitchens or laboratories will sit at the top of this list and be running perfectly well. What the ranking does is order your estate so that the next hour of investigation goes to the building where it is worth most — and the out-of-hours review is what answers "is this waste or is this the job" for any one of them.
