Where to look first

Five places, in the order a facilities team would check them.

1

The off step, and who is in the building after hours

Load stays up until 22.0, 4.0 hours past 18:00. Cleaning rounds and a late off step are the two usual causes, and one phone call to the cleaning contractor tells you which.

2

What is actually drawing the overnight load

Something holds 71 kW through the night. This report can say how much and when, not what. Server and comms rooms, lifts, external and car-park lighting, refrigeration and car-park ventilation are where it usually sits. A week with a clamp meter on the main sub-circuits settles it for good.

3

The days the building was supposed to be empty

24,853 kWh (2,734 EUR) was used across the 9 closed dates you listed. Those are the cheapest days to trial a change, because nothing that happens on them can inconvenience anybody.

These are places to check, not conclusions. This review reads the meter; it has not seen your plant. Each item names what the data shows and the usual cause, so whoever knows the building can confirm or rule it out in an afternoon.
